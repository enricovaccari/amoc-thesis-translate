SCENE I — THE COLUMN
The Column
The Atlantic moves as a vertical structure: warm water flowing north near the surface, colder water returning south in the deep. This is not a single current but a whole column, and that column can strengthen or weaken. Below, the real shape of that transport at 26°N — and what remains when we keep only its two dominant modes.
Here you can switch condition (C1·C2·C3), scrub the months, and slide through depth — in C3, hover the column and listen to its pulse.
